FAQ

Before you book.

Will I lose my games or saves?
No. Only the new SSD is formatted. Everything on your PS5's internal storage stays as it is, and you can move games to the new drive from Settings → Storage.
Does it work with the PS5 Slim and PS5 Pro?
Yes. The original PS5, the PS5 Slim and the PS5 Pro all have an M.2 expansion slot. Tell us your model when you book.
