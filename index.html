FAQ

Before you book.

Will I lose my games or saves?

No. Only the new SSD is formatted. Everything on your PS5's internal storage stays as it is, and you can move games to the new drive from Settings → Storage.

Does it work with the PS5 Slim and PS5 Pro?

Yes. The original PS5, the PS5 Slim and the PS5 Pro all have an M.2 expansion slot. Tell us your model when you book.

What does the price include?

Everything: the Kingston NV3 drive, pickup, installation, formatting, testing and delivery back to you. No hidden fees.

What if something goes wrong?

You don't pay until your PS5 is back and working. The drive has the manufacturer's warranty, our installation has a 3-month warranty, and if we damage your console we repair or replace it.
